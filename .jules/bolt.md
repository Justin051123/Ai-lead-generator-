## 2026-06-23 - Parallelizing I/O-Bound Lead Scraping and Regex Pre-compilation
**Learning:** Moving from sequential lead extraction with sleep timers to parallel lead scraping via concurrent ThreadPoolExecutor significantly reduces overall scraping time (from 6.00s down to 0.20s for 5 leads in a mocked environment, a 30x improvement). Pre-compiling the email extraction regex pattern also optimizes processing throughput. Using `executor.map` ensures that the search result relevance order is preserved.
**Action:** Always prefer `ThreadPoolExecutor` for network/I/O-heavy scraping tasks where multiple external pages are being requested, and use `executor.map` over `as_completed` if result order/ranking must be maintained. Pre-compile regular expressions that are reused in loops.
