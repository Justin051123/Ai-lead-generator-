## 2026-06-23 - Parallel Lead Processing with ThreadPoolExecutor
**Learning:** Sequential scraping with artificial delays introduces significant I/O-bound wait times that scale linearly with the number of leads. Since requests to separate websites are completely independent and I/O-bound, we can execute them concurrently using a `ThreadPoolExecutor`. By utilizing `executor.map`, we preserve the original ranking and order returned by the Google Search API, maintaining lead relevance while removing artificial sequential sleeps. Additionally, compiling regex patterns once at the module level avoids redundant compilation overhead in hot loops.
**Action:** Always parallelize web-scraping/I/O-bound tasks in Python where ordering is critical by using `concurrent.futures.ThreadPoolExecutor` paired with `executor.map`.
